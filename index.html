Looking for an Omegle alternative that is actually a game?

If you want a random face-to-face format with rules and a winner, Omoggle Game is a free browser 1v1 mog battle with on-device PSL scoring, audience votes and ELO ranking.

Many people who used to open random video chat sites were really after the quick, unpredictable matchup. A game format adds structure: each round has two players, a score, a vote and a result that changes your rating. That is the idea behind omoggle game, a browser-based mog battle.

What makes it different from open-ended chat

  • Rounds are 1v1 battles, not endless conversations.
  • A PSL-style score is computed on-device in your browser.
  • The audience votes on the winner.
  • Your results are tracked with an ELO rating.
  • It is free and runs in the browser.

FAQ

Do I need to install anything? No, it is a browser game.

What decides a round? Audience votes, with outcomes feeding ELO.

Is this omoggle.com? No. omogglegame.com is independent and not affiliated with omoggle.com.
